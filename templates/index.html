Police

Helpline: 100

Connects you to police support for threats, harassment, stalking, assault, or unsafe situations.

Best for: Immediate law enforcement help and rapid local response.

Ambulance

Helpline: 108

Used for urgent medical emergencies such as injuries, fainting, severe pain, or accidents.

Best for: Fast medical transport and emergency health assistance.

Women Helpline

Helpline: 181 / 1091

Supports women facing violence, abuse, intimidation, or needing guidance and coordinated assistance.

Best for: Safety support, counselling, referrals, and women-focused emergency help.

Emergency Response

Helpline: 112

A single emergency number that can route urgent requests for police, medical, fire, and rescue support.

Best for: One-number emergency help when you need the fastest coordination.

Cyber Crime

Helpline: 1930

For online fraud, scam payments, suspicious account activity, blackmail, or digital safety complaints.

Best for: Reporting cyber fraud quickly to improve the chance of stopping losses.

Child Helpline

Helpline: 1098

Provides support for children in danger, abuse, neglect, trafficking, or urgent shelter needs.

Best for: Immediate intervention and protection for children at risk.
